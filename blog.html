Professional Biohazard Cleanup: A Comprehensive Guide

May 1, 2025

Professional biohazard cleanup demands specialized expertise and strict adherence to safety protocols. Technicians must utilize comprehensive personal protective equipment, including respirators, multiple glove layers, and protective suits. The process begins with thorough scene assessment and documentation, followed by systematic cleaning procedures involving contaminated material removal, sanitization, and disinfection. Proper waste disposal complying with regulations is crucial, using approved containers and maintaining detailed records. Quality assurance measures, including ATP testing and UV inspection, ensure spaces are safe for occupancy. This meticulous approach protects both cleanup technicians and future occupants while meeting all regulatory requirements.
