About Us

Founded in 2010, CleanScene emerged from a commitment to provide professional, compassionate cleanup services during life's most challenging moments. Our founder's personal experience with tragedy led to the creation of a company that combines technical expertise with genuine empathy.

Our mission is to restore safety and peace of mind to affected spaces while treating every situation with the utmost respect and discretion. We uphold the core values of integrity, professionalism, and compassion in every service we provide, ensuring that families and businesses can focus on healing while we handle the technical aspects of cleanup and restoration.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your response time?

We guarantee arrival within 2 hours anywhere in our service area, 24/7/365. Our emergency response team is always ready to deploy.

How do you handle confidentiality?

We maintain strict confidentiality protocols, including unmarked vehicles, plain clothes technicians, and secure documentation procedures. All staff sign comprehensive NDAs.

What certifications do your technicians have?

Our technicians hold ABRA certification, OSHA bloodborne pathogen certification, and are trained in advanced biohazard cleanup procedures.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Yes, we work directly with all major insurance providers and can handle all necessary documentation and claims processing on your behalf.

What areas do you service?

We provide services throughout the entire metropolitan area and surrounding regions within a 100-mile radius of our headquarters.
